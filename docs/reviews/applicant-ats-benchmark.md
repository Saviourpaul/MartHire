# Applicant and candidate profile ATS benchmark

Research note comparing official Workable and Greenhouse documentation for application-form collection and candidate profiles. **Accessed 2026-11-22**; sources below are first-party help-center pages. Dates are the publication/update dates shown by the page when available. For Workable pages that did not expose an update date, that is called out rather than inferred.

## Findings

### Workable

- **Application form and screening.** Default fields on each job form can be mandatory, optional, or off. Workable custom questions are required and are shown in a separate section of the candidate profile; question answers can be used to filter candidates and included in the Candidate details report. Supported custom-question types cover paragraph, short answer, yes/no, dropdown, multiple choice, date, number, and file upload. The docs distinguish these from custom fields, which can have restricted visibility for sensitive information. [Customizing the application form](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012231948-Customizing-the-application-form) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed); [What types of questions can I add to my application form?](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012087467-What-types-of-questions-can-I-add-to-my-application-form) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed).
- **Resume parsing and profile structure.** Resume uploads are parsed for name, email/contact information, social-media account links, and profile picture, then copied into the candidate profile when found. Workable documents image-based and multilingual CV support and manual editing after parse. Separately, its Candidate details report exposes social profiles, education entries, experience entries, skills, and custom fields as profile/report data; the source does not say all those fields are extracted from resumes by the parser. [Uploading candidate resumes / CVs: Individual and bulk options](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012661408-Uploading-candidate-resumes-CVs-Individual-and-bulk-options) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed); [Using the Candidate details report](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012920428-Using-the-Candidate-details-report) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed).
- **Profile links and photos.** Workable says it can find public social links and photos on the web and add them to applicant/sourced profiles by default. These settings are optional; admins can choose to collect links without showing them or limit photos to candidate-submitted ones. [Managing candidate photos and social media profiles](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011872207-Managing-candidate-photos-and-social-media-profiles) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed).
- **Diversity and equal-opportunity data.** Workable separates application screening questions from post-application candidate surveys. It describes surveys as optional and anonymous for D&I/demographic data not used to assess role suitability. Its U.S.-only EEO/OFCCP survey is voluntary; depending on the application source, candidates may see it after applying or during Indeed Apply. EEO questions cover race/ethnicity and gender; federal-contractor OFCCP sections separately cover veteran and disability self-identification. The fixed U.S. questions are not editable. The collection-methods article, dated 2024-10-21 and marked “Updated” without a visible revised date, also distinguishes private custom fields, visible screening questions, anonymous candidate surveys, and the Compliance report. [When should I use a survey question instead of an application form question?](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500003370142-When-should-I-use-a-survey-question-instead-of-an-application-form-question) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed); [Enabling EEO/OFCCP options](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011841267-Enabling-EEO-OFCCP-options) (accessed 2026-11-22; update date not displayed); [Collecting candidate info: what methods are available](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/17174909579287-Collecting-candidate-info-what-methods-are-available) (published 2024-10-21; marked updated, revised date not displayed; accessed 2026-11-22).

### Greenhouse

- **Reusable candidate profile and autofill.** MyGreenhouse lets candidates keep a reusable profile containing a resume, contact info, employment history, education, skills, online profiles, work preferences, voluntary self-identification, and an “About you” section. Aside from the account email, the candidate FAQ says profile fields are optional and editable/deletable. Quick Apply uses the resume to autofill the employer’s application, including its questions; the candidate reviews and edits answers before submission. Greenhouse warns parsing can make mistakes. [MyGreenhouse FAQ for Candidates](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/43418495049499-MyGreenhouse-FAQ-for-Candidates) (last updated 2026-11-02; accessed 2026-11-22); [MyGreenhouse Quick Apply](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/35746094035099-MyGreenhouse-Quick-Apply) (last updated 2026-10-30; accessed 2026-11-22).
- **Structured profile and parse boundaries.** Greenhouse’s Talent Matching data-processing FAQ says that feature extracts resume-derived skills, job titles, years of experience, employment start/end dates, and company names as structured data; its matching calculation does not currently include structured application-form answers. This is documentation for the Talent Matching feature specifically, not a blanket claim about every Greenhouse resume-parsing path. [Talent Matching - Data Processing FAQ](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41131616864283-Talent-Matching-Data-Processing-FAQ) (last updated 2026-02-02; accessed 2026-11-22). The candidate-profile overview also says custom application answers appear in candidate details and the profile includes application history across jobs. [Candidate profile redesign overview](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/30352015432987-Candidate-profile-redesign-overview) (last updated 2026-03-20; accessed 2026-11-22).
- **Custom application questions.** Job posts can define the question, optional description, answer type, privacy setting, and whether it is required. Answers are visible under the application section. To make responses structured, searchable, and reportable across jobs, a question can be linked to a candidate field; supported linked field types are short/long text, yes/no, single-select, and multi-select. Unlinked custom answers are reportable per job; linking is needed for cross-job reporting. [Add a custom application question to a job post](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360025222851-Add-a-custom-application-question-to-a-job-post) (last updated 2026-03-02; accessed 2026-11-22); [Link custom job post question to custom field on candidate profile](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360024688071-Link-custom-job-post-question-to-custom-field-on-candidate-profile) (last updated 2026-03-02; accessed 2026-11-22).
- **Diversity and equal-opportunity data.** Greenhouse offers optional, preconfigured U.S. standard demographic questions that can be placed on job posts or candidate surveys. The standard-question report does not link responses to individual candidates, and suppresses exact counts for categories with fewer than five hires. A separate custom demographic-question feature allows configurable question sets; configured questions must be added to job posts or surveys for candidates to see them. Greenhouse explicitly says its standard demographic set does not replace EEOC questions where legally required and advises customer legal review. [U.S. Standard demographic questions](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/7541739987355-U-S-Standard-demographic-questions) (last updated 2026-07-31; accessed 2026-11-22); [Custom demographic questions](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360004588971-Custom-demographic-questions) (last updated 2026-03-02; accessed 2026-11-22).

## Product recommendations (inferences from the sources)

1. Keep resume upload and parsed data together, but make extracted fields editable and reviewable. Treat parsing as a convenience, not a source of truth.
2. Provide normalized, optional candidate-profile sections for employment, education, skills, and profile links. Preserve the original resume, and make the source of each profile value clear (candidate-entered, parsed, or imported) if that distinction is available.
3. Separate reusable profile data from per-job screening answers. Offer typed question controls, required/optional choice, and a deliberate way to map reusable answers into searchable profile fields.
4. Use the least data necessary for screening. Keep diversity/equal-opportunity collection separate from evaluative questions; make it optional where appropriate, restrict who can view individual responses, and report aggregate results. The cited U.S.-specific workflows should not be treated as jurisdiction-neutral compliance rules.
5. Make social/photo enrichment opt-in or hidden by default unless a clear recruiting need justifies it. Workable documents a way to collect links without displaying them and to show only candidate-submitted photos.

## Scope note

This is a product-pattern benchmark, not legal advice or a claim that the vendors' data fields, availability, pricing tiers, and compliance workflows are identical. Features can vary by plan, region, application channel, and administrator configuration; validate the relevant configuration against current product documentation before implementation.
