## What Is an Applicant Tracking System? The MartHire Approach

## MartHire — Recruitment Solution
What is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?
An Applicant Tracking System is software that brings the hiring process into one place: from creating a vacancy to selecting a candidate. Instead of spreadsheets, inboxes, and scattered documents, recruiters and hiring teams work from a single structured workflow.
MartHire is a recruitment technology platform built around this idea. It is not a job board. Its purpose is to help organizations manage hiring from start to finish, with applicant tracking at the core.

## How MartHire is designed to work
MartHire connects every stage of hiring into one workflow:
Organization → Recruitment Campaign → Job Vacancy → Applications → Screening → Shortlisting → Interview → Evaluation → Selection
1. Vacancy creation and publication. Employers and recruiters create a vacancy with the job title, description, responsibilities, requirements, location, employment type, and application deadline. Where an organization requires it, the vacancy is reviewed and approved before it becomes publicly available.
2. Candidate applications. Candidates create a profile, search vacancies, upload the required documents, and apply online. Their application is stored securely and linked to their profile, so they do not have to start from scratch each time.
3. Screening and shortlisting. Recruiters review applicants in one place, filter them against the vacancy requirements, and move qualified candidates forward. Each application moves through clear stages: Submitted, Shortlisted, Interview, Selected, or Rejected, with Withdrawn available to candidates. Every stage change is recorded in a status history, so decisions can be traced.
4. Candidate management. MartHire keeps a central record for each candidate: personal and contact details, education, qualifications, skills, work experience, CV, supporting documents, and application history. Recruiters can search and retrieve this information without digging through email threads.
5. Interviews and evaluation. Recruiters manage interviews and evaluate candidates within the same workflow, so feedback stays attached to the application it belongs to.
6. Reporting. Organizations get practical recruitment reports: active vacancies, new applications, candidates awaiting review, shortlisted candidates, interviews, and selections. Over time, this supports measures such as time-to-fill and recruitment funnel analysis.

## Why organizations choose a structured recruitment workflow
•	Simpler and faster. Repetitive tasks such as sorting applications, updating statuses, and notifying candidates are handled by the system, giving recruiters more time for actual hiring decisions.
•	Transparent for candidates. Candidates can see where they are in the process and receive meaningful notifications, instead of waiting without feedback.
•	More organized for recruiters. Applicants, vacancies, and recruitment campaigns are managed in a structured way rather than across disconnected tools.
•	Traceable decisions. Status history and audit trails give organizations a clear record of how each hiring decision progressed.
•	Built to grow. Whether an organization is filling one role or running many campaigns at once, the platform is designed to scale with its hiring volume.
•	Configurable. Organizations can shape the workflow around their own hiring process rather than adapting to a fixed template.
Who MartHire serves
•	Employers and organizations: companies, SMEs, NGOs, institutions, and government bodies managing their own recruitment.
•	Recruiters and recruitment agencies: teams handling multiple recruitment processes and clients.
•	Candidates: job seekers who want a simple, mobile-friendly, and transparent application experience.
•	Administrators: the people who manage users, moderation, and the platform itself.
For developers and integration partners

MartHire is built on a clear data model so that recruitment data stays consistent and, in the future, easy to connect with other systems. 
## The core objects are:
•	Organizations: employer accounts and their recruiters.
•	Recruitment Campaigns: a hiring effort that can contain one or more vacancies.
•	Job Posts: the vacancies, with their details and publication status.
•	Candidates: profiles, qualifications, skills, and CVs.
•	Applications: a candidate's submission to a specific vacancy.
•	Application Status History: the recorded movement of each application through the pipeline.
•	Documents: CVs, cover letters, and supporting files, protected by access control.
•	Interviews: schedules and interview details.
•	Evaluations and activity records: structured candidate evaluations and recruiter notes, planned as the platform matures.

## future Implementation
An API and third-party integrations are part of the long-term direction, not the launch scope. Possible future uses include:
•	Recruitment analytics dashboards across an organization's campaigns.
•	Interview scheduling and calendar coordination.
•	Email, SMS, and WhatsApp notifications to candidates.
•	Connecting verification or assessment services to specific pipeline stages.
•	AI-assisted screening, introduced only where it can be explained and fairly applied.
